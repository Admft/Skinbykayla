# SkinByKaylaa SEO + AI Search Playbook

This playbook is written for a real small local business, not a fake keyword-stuffed site. The goal is to help Google, Bing, Maps, and AI assistants understand exactly who SkinByKaylaa is, where it is, what it offers, and why clients trust the studio.

## 1. The local SEO foundation

### NAP must match everywhere

NAP means name, address, phone.

Use this everywhere exactly:

- Name: SkinByKaylaa
- Address: 305 E Main St, Royse City, TX 75189-3726
- Phone: (214) 612-8225
- Website: https://www.skinbykaylaa.com/

- Website footer
- Google Business Profile
- Bing Places

- Facebook

3. Add every service with the same name, description, price, and duration as the website.
4. Add photos of the treatment room, storefront, products, brow results, lash lift results, and aftercare setup.
5. Ask clients for reviews after good appointments.
6. Reply to every review naturally.
7. Post updates monthly: openings, seasonal reminders, brow/lash tips, waxing prep tips.

## 3. The review strategy

The reviews are visible on the site because reviews build trust. The site does not add review star schema to the business because that can be considered self-serving review markup for local businesses.

What to do instead:

- Keep reviews on the page for human trust.
- Link to the Google review form.
- Ask every happy client for a Google review.
- Reply to reviews using service terms naturally.

Example reply:

> Thank you so much for trusting SkinByKaylaa for your Brazilian wax in Royse City. I am so glad the appointment felt comfortable and easy for you.

Do not copy-paste the same reply every time.

## 6. AI search / answer engine optimization

This project includes:

- `/llms.txt`
- `/ai-search-profile/`
- clear NAP in page text
- schema.org structured data
- service pages with natural intent phrases

This helps answer engines parse the business, but it does not guarantee that ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews will recommend the business. AI recommendations are based on many signals: web content, reviews, citations, maps data, recency, authority, and user location.

To improve AI visibility:

1. Keep Google Business Profile accurate.
2. Get more Google reviews mentioning real services.
3. Build consistent citations across trusted local directories.
4. Publish clear service pages.
5. Keep llms.txt updated.
6. Add photos with descriptive filenames before upload.
7. Make sure the same business facts appear everywhere.

## 11. Things not to do

Do not:

- Hide lists of keywords in tiny text.
- Add fake reviews.
- Use review schema for self-controlled local business reviews.
- Make medical claims.
- Say SkinByKaylaa cures acne or treats medical skin conditions.
- Claim to be the best unless the claim is backed by a verifiable award or source.
- Copy competitor text.
- Use the same city/service paragraph on every page.
